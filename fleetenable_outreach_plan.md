## LinkedIn DM Tips

1. **Only DM if connected or if InMail** — you need to be connected or have Premium
2. **Keep it under 300 characters** — people read DMs on mobile
3. **Lead with value** — "I built X / I have Y experience"
4. **Include a clear ask** — "Open to a quick chat?"
5. **If not connected yet:** Send a connection request with a note:
   - *"Hi [Name] — I was Tech Lead at FleetEnable (drayage TMS). Exploring roles at [Company]. Would love to connect."*
6. **Never send a resume in the first message** — that's what applications are for

## Email Follow-up After LinkedIn DM

If they respond positively to DM on LinkedIn, follow up via email with your resume:
- *"Great to connect here! Just sent my resume via email as well — jrohit072@gmail.com. Happy to hop on a call this week."*

If they don't respond in 5-7 days, follow up on LinkedIn:
- *"Hi [Name] — just a gentle follow-up on my previous message. I know you're busy. Happy to share my resume if helpful."*
