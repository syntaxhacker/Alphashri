### What the fleet proved (evidence-backed)
- **On NQ the FVG/iFVG *retrace* entry is the real, repeatable edge** (J, K, L, M, O all positive both regimes). **iFVG (K)** is the strongest and fastest prop passer (~12-day median at 0.5%).
- **The pure-ICT *confluence* is decoration on top of the FVG**: liquidity-sweep context is noise-to-harmful as a filter (Q −0.06…−0.09R; P proves removing sweep+trend filters *raises* edge; S null; U fails). The FVG is the **entry**, not a filter.
- **More/bigger filters ≠ better**: only ~3 of 8 filter families add EV (high-vol regime, mid-session, displacement); big gap size, London, daily-open side, and sweeps actively **hurt** (Q).
- **Higher R:R hurts** on the HTF FVG (T: only RR2 survives); the big winners come from **trailing/time exits** (v3, v1), not far fixed targets.
- **Longs ≫ shorts** throughout (O, S, T all agree); **continuation ≫ reversal**.

### Unifying finding (evidence-backed)
**ICT *reversal* concepts are noise on NQ; *continuation* is where the edge lives.**
- Sweeps tend to **CONTINUE**, not reverse (agents E, I both found continuation > reversal; the raw LRL/G/London/Asia reversal versions were negative).
- Every robust winner is a **breakout / momentum-continuation** with a trend/bias filter: v3 (1h breakout), ORB family, and G (sweep taken **in the daily-bias direction**, +3R).
- Time-only filters (kill zones) add nothing (H).

**Conclusion:** the prop-worthy edges are **v3 (+0.42R)** and **G (+0.35R, low freq)**. Pass and print with **v3**.
