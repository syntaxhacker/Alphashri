## 🔧 Technical Implementation

### **Multiple Strategies Implemented**
1. **Momentum Breakout**
   - 5-day high breakout
   - Volume confirmation (>20% above average)
   - RSI momentum zone (45-80)

2. **Mean Reversion**
   - Below lower Bollinger Band
   - RSI oversold (<35)
   - Not at 10-day low

3. **MACD Bullish Crossover**
   - MACD crosses above signal line
   - Volume confirmation
   - Not deeply negative

4. **Golden Cross Mini**
   - 10-day SMA crosses above 20-day SMA
   - Volume confirmation
   - **Best performing strategy**

### **Smart Position Sizing**
- Risk-based position sizing (3-5% of capital per trade)
- Strategy-specific stop losses (4-7%)
- Maximum 30% capital exposure per trade

### **Advanced Exit Strategies**
- Strategy-specific profit targets (8-15%)
- Technical exits (Bollinger Band mean reversion)
